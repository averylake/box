The Work Two states of one idea: sealed, and too alive to stay sealed.

The Alphabet Is the Box exists in two parts, shown side by side above. The alphabet appears in both, not simply as language, but as a capture technology: a system that makes thought transmissible by giving it a boundary. The boundary is useful. It is also not the thought.

Incubation begins with an idea already alive. It breathes behind three small openings in a container built entirely from letters. The container never opens. It only breathes.

Emergence is what happens next. The container cannot hold what has grown inside it. The lid lifts, the letters disperse, and what remains is neither explained nor translated. It moves. The work ends without a word.

Artist Statement Before a thought becomes language, something has already happened.
Language
Capture
The uncaptured

I make work about the media we live inside without noticing, the way we live inside weather.

This piece began with a recurring thought: language puts our ideas into a box. It is an extraordinarily useful box. It lets a thought travel, lets one mind reach another. But it is still a box, and every box flattens and reduces what it holds. Language is so close to how we think, so familiar that we stop noticing it as a technology at all, that we start treating the word as though it were the equivalent of the thought. It is not.

Writing and AI belong to the same longer lineage of capture. Each turns what is moving, situated, and partly unknowable into something that can circulate. Capture is not only loss; it lets us remember, share, and build worlds together. But every capture also makes a border, and something always remains before or beyond it.

This work asks what an idea might look like before the alphabet gives it a name, and what might happen if it becomes too alive for that name to hold.

03
Lineage The box that once protected imagination becomes the alphabet that contains it.
The Little Prince
Alphabet
AI

The work begins from the paradox of the sheep in The Little Prince. A literal drawing fails, while a simple box succeeds because it leaves the sheep unseen and allows imagination to complete it.

The Alphabet Is the Box reverses that permission. Its box is made from the alphabet itself. What first appears to contain an idea gradually reveals itself as the system that captures it.

The work unfolds in two states. Incubation holds the box sealed: three openings, a breathing form, no resolution. Emergence is what happens when the idea will not stay contained.

The work sits beside The Alphabet That Answers and the larger field of Signatures Through Technologies, where alphabet, code, AI, light, and silence are treated as successive structures through which the human signal passes.
